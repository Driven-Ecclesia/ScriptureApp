How to Use This With Google Meet

1. Click "Open Display Window" — a second, clean window opens showing only the scripture (no controls).
2. In Google Meet, click Present now → A window, and select that Display window specifically (not your whole screen).
3. Come back to this control panel — search, preview, and click "Send to Display". Your congregation only ever sees the clean display window updating; they never see this control panel.
4. If you look up a range of verses (e.g. Romans 8:1-4), only one verse shows on the display at a time — use the Prev / Next buttons in the preview to step through the passage and send each verse when you're ready for it.
5. Want NIV, NLT, Amplified, or similar? Register a free app key at platform.youversion.com (recommended — free, non-commercial, includes NIV), paste it into "More translations," and click Load Translations.
